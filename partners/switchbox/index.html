The partnership

What each partner brings

A governed AI platform, and an engineering team that builds it into the systems clients already run.

  • NeuralSeek brings the platform

    Answers grounded in the organization's own sources, agent workflows, runtime guardrails and a record of every run, as SaaS or in a container on the organization's own infrastructure.

  • Switchbox brings the engineering

    Two decades of custom software, legacy modernization, systems integration and AI enablement: LLM integration, RAG architecture, agentic workflows and human-in-the-loop design.

Governance in practice

AI agents that hold up in production

Every answer is scored and every run is logged, so teams can see what an agent did, which sources it used and how confident it was. Switchbox designs permissions, integrations and review steps around that record.

Common questions about the partnership

What teams ask before they start a project with Switchbox and NeuralSeek.

  1. 01

    What is the NeuralSeek and Switchbox partnership?

    +

    Switchbox is a NeuralSeek partner. NeuralSeek provides the governed AI platform, and Switchbox designs, integrates and deploys AI agents on it for its clients.

  2. 02

    What does Switchbox do?

    +

    Switchbox is a custom software and AI company based in Columbus, Ohio, with a 100% U.S.-based team and about 20 years of experience. It offers product strategy, AI enablement, custom application development, legacy modernization, systems integration, data and analytics, and cloud operations.

  3. 03

    Which industries does the partnership serve?

    +

    Healthcare, government and manufacturing.

  4. 04

    Can NeuralSeek run on our own infrastructure?

    +

    Yes. NeuralSeek runs as SaaS or in a container on the organization's own infrastructure. Switchbox can integrate it with existing systems in either setup.

  5. 05

    How does NeuralSeek make AI agents auditable?

    +

    Every answer is grounded in the organization's own sources, scored for confidence and logged, so each run can be reviewed later.

  6. 06

    Why do AI agents fail in production, and how does this partnership address it?

    +

    Many agents work in demos but stall when they meet sensitive data, real users and audit requirements. Switchbox designs the controls and integrations up front, and NeuralSeek grounds, scores and logs every answer, so the agent can be reviewed and trusted after launch.

  7. 07

    How do we start a project with Switchbox and NeuralSeek?

    +

    Most projects start with one use case. Talk to NeuralSeek sales, or contact Switchbox directly through switchboxinc.com.
