Consulting and regional partners

How delivery partners work with us

Consulting firms deliver NeuralSeek to their own clients, often in regulated sectors, and regional partners bring it to new markets and languages.

  1. 01

    Speed

    A working use case goes live quickly, inside the client's existing access controls and audit requirements, so the first engagement delivers a running system.

  2. 02

    Simplicity

    Compliance, risk and operations teams build and manage workflows without waiting on engineering, so a partner can hand the deployment over to the client.

  3. 03

    Success

    Every answer carries its source, every run can be audited, and the platform works across structured and unstructured data, so what the partner delivers stands up to the client's next audit.
