Where NeuralSeek fits

How NeuralSeek runs on IBM Fusion

Your data center holds the rack. IBM Fusion provides the hardware and the container platform, NeuralSeek runs on that platform, and your teams use what is built on top. Every layer sits in the same room, so your data stays there too.

  • Your assistants and agents

    Search, answers, drafting and multi-step agents, the tools your teams open every day.

  • NeuralSeek

    Retrieval over your documents, agents, guardrails and governance. The same container that runs in the cloud, here on your hardware.

  • Red Hat OpenShift

    The container platform NeuralSeek installs onto. IBM describes Fusion as pre-configured infrastructure for OpenShift, so it is ready on arrival.

  • IBM Fusion HCI

    Compute, storage and networking in one system, with GPU-powered nodes for inference, in your data center beside your identity provider and your data.

How it is deployed

From rack to answers

Your documents, prompts and logs stay inside your building at every step.

  1. 01

    The Fusion rack goes into your data center

    IBM Fusion HCI arrives as pre-configured infrastructure for Red Hat OpenShift: compute, storage, networking and GPU nodes in one system. It joins your network and identity provider like any other system.

  2. 02

    NeuralSeek is installed on OpenShift

    NeuralSeek on-prem runs as containers on OpenShift or Kubernetes, so it goes onto the cluster Fusion already provides, with no extra servers to buy.

  3. 03

    Connect your documents and a model

    Point it at the document stores and systems your teams already use, and at the language model you choose. The index lives on the appliance, next to the sources.

  4. 04

    Build, govern and run

    Teams build assistants and agents in the console. Guardrails check every answer and every run is logged on the appliance, which can run fully air-gapped.

We scope sizing and installation for your environment, starting from what you already run.

Governed on arrival

Guardrails come with the appliance

The rules for what an answer may say, and what it must refuse, run in the same unit in your data center. You configure them in the product and they apply on every run.

What NeuralSeek adds

What NeuralSeek adds to Fusion

Fusion supplies the infrastructure. NeuralSeek adds retrieval, agents, guardrails and governance.

Every capability above ships in the same container wherever it runs. The appliance gives it a home in your data center.

  • Retrieval over your own documents

    Answers grounded in the knowledge base you connect, with confidence and coverage metrics and provenance on every answer. The sources and the index stay on the appliance.

    Semantic retrieval

  • Agents built on the platform

    Multi-step workflows that query documents, databases and APIs, written in NeuralSeek's template language without traditional coding.

    Agent workflows

  • Guardrails on every request

    Screening, redaction and grounding checks run inside the appliance before and after every model call.

    Guardrails

  • Governance you can show an examiner

    Dashboards for confidence, coverage, usage and cost, and a log of what was asked, retrieved and answered, kept on the appliance and exportable to the collector you already run.

    Governance

For work that stays on site

One appliance for regulated industries. Records stay on the appliance and off public networks.

    Patient data stays in the hospital

    For hospitals, payers and the teams that write clinical and medical-education content.
