# One global group, deliberately. Per the robots spec, user-agent groups are
# NOT combined — only the most specific matching group applies. Naming ~20 bots
# each with "Allow: /" buys nothing (crawling is allowed by default) and arms a
# footgun: the day someone adds a Disallow here, every named bot ignores it.
#
# Google-Extended and Applebot-Extended are deliberately NOT blocked. They gate
# AI grounding and citation, not Search ranking. For an AI company that wants to
# be cited by AI, being crawlable is the point.
#
# Content-Signal (contentsignals.org) says the same thing in words: search,
# AI answers (ai-input) and model training (ai-train) are all allowed. We want
# to be cited by assistants and known by the models themselves — a decision by
# the site owner, not a default. It sits inside the one group, as the spec asks.
#
# No Agentmap line (Oct 8 2026): Lighthouse reports it as an unknown directive
# and fails the file. Agents find the catalog at /.well-known/ard.json (and the
# older /.well-known/ai-catalog.json) and through <link rel="ard"> on every page.

User-agent: *
Content-Signal: search=yes, ai-input=yes, ai-train=yes
Allow: /
