Languages

Answers in the language of the question

Spanish, Portuguese and English answers come from the same verified knowledge base, so staff and members get the same answer in whichever language they ask.

  • Spanish, Portuguese, English and more

    Supported languages include English, Spanish and Portuguese alongside French, German, Italian, Arabic, Korean, Chinese, Japanese and others. One knowledge base serves all of them.

  • Language detected automatically

    NeuralSeek detects the language of each question and answers in it, with no setup per language. A Spanish question gets a Spanish answer, even from content written in English.

  • More languages with Match Input

    Other languages are supported through the Match Input setting.

Questions before a pilot

Short answers to what institutions in the region ask first.

  1. 01

    Can processing stay in our country?

    +

    Yes. Run the NeuralSeek container on Kubernetes or OpenShift in your own cloud account, data center or air-gapped site. Retrieval, orchestration and the audit trail run there with it.

  2. 02

    Does a question have to leave the country to reach a model?

    +

    Only if you choose a model hosted elsewhere. Everything else runs in your deployment. Use your own provider keys or an open model on your own hardware, and the model call stays in your environment too.

  3. 03

    Our content is in English. Our members ask in Spanish.

    +

    One knowledge base serves both. NeuralSeek detects the language of the question and answers in it, with no second copy of the content. Portuguese works the same way.
