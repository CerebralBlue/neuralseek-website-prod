Use cases

What healthcare teams build

Every use case follows the same steps: mask sensitive data, search approved material, answer, check and log.

  • Clinical questions from staff

    Sensitive data is masked first. The answer comes from content your organization approved, is checked against your confidence minimum, cites its source and is logged.

  • Document intake and summaries

    Read a referral packet or scanned chart, extract keywords, match them to your provider and specialty records, and return a drafted summary. Scanning, matching and the audit record are steps in one flow.

  • Expert-in-the-loop authoring

    Research and drafting help for specialists who write clinical and educational material. Accuracy rules run as guardrails, with human review built in.

  • Administrative and member answers

    Benefits, coverage, scheduling and process questions answered from one verified knowledge base, on every channel. Every reply is screened for harmful language and sensitive data.

Privacy and security

Privacy controls in every deployment

Data protection, encryption, audit logs and no training on your data, in every deployment, including our SaaS.

  • PII detection and redaction

    Flag, mask, hide or delete personal data, configured by topic.

  • Encrypted in transit and at rest

    TLS 1.2+ on all network traffic. Data at rest under AES-256. NeuralSeek integrates with your own key management service.

  • Full audit trail

    Every question, answer, score and settings change is logged, timestamped and attributed. Export it to S3, Splunk or Datadog.

  • No training on your data

    Your data stays yours. Neither NeuralSeek nor its subprocessors use your prompts, documents or logs to train models.

Questions from privacy teams

Short answers to what a privacy office asks first.

  1. 01

    What actually reaches the model?

    +

    Only what you configure. Sensitive data is detected before the model call, and you choose the action by topic: mask, flag, hide or delete. To keep the model inside too, run an open-weight model on your own hardware.

  2. 02

    Can it run inside our own network?

    +

    Yes. It runs as SaaS, in your cloud account, in a sovereign region, on OpenShift or fully air-gapped on-prem, with the same redaction, guardrails and audit controls everywhere.

  3. 03

    How do we know an answer came from our approved material?

    +

    Every answer carries the passages it came from and a confidence score. Answers below your minimum get your own reply instead. Each run can be replayed, and the record exports to your log system.
