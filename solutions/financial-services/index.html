Security

What your security review checks first

These controls come with every deployment, including our SaaS.

  • PII detection and redaction

    Flag, mask, hide or delete personal data, configured by topic.

  • Full audit trail

    Every question, answer, score and settings change is logged, timestamped and attributed. Export it to S3, Splunk or Datadog.

  • Per-tenant isolation

    Separate keys per tenant. Your prompts, documents and outputs stay in your tenant and are never used to train models.

  • No training on your data

    Your data stays yours. Neither NeuralSeek nor its subprocessors use your prompts, documents or logs to train models.

Questions from risk and compliance teams

Short answers to what decides whether a project goes live.

  1. 01

    Can you show us why it answered that way?

    +

    Every answer carries its source passages and a confidence score. Each run can be replayed step by step: agent, model, output and timing. Every settings change is logged and can be undone, and logs export to the collector you already run.

  2. 02

    Our data must stay in our tenant.

    +

    NeuralSeek runs in your own account, region or data center, with search, agents and logs alongside it. Bring your own model keys, or run an open-weight model on your hardware to keep the model inside too. Air-gapped deployment is standard.

  3. 03

    We already have a model provider and a team building on it.

    +

    NeuralSeek works with the model you already use and adds search, guardrails and a full audit trail. Changing provider later is a settings change, and your apps stay as they are.
