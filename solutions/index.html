What you get

What every regulated team needs

Every industry uses different terms, but the requirements are the same. NeuralSeek covers all of them.

  • Answers you can trace

    Answers come from your own content, with a citation back to the source passage and a confidence score. A weakly grounded answer goes to a fallback instead of the user.

  • Guardrails on every request

    Hundreds of built-in guardrails check every question and answer, and mask sensitive data before it reaches the model.

  • A record of every answer

    Every question, answer and score is timestamped and can be exported to the log collector you already run. Every settings change is logged and can be undone.

  • Your data stays where you put it

    Run it as SaaS, in your own cloud account, a sovereign region, OpenShift or air-gapped on-prem. Same guardrails, audit trail and features everywhere.
