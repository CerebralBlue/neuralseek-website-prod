Use cases

What energy and utility teams can build

  • 01

    Answers for field crews

    Crews ask in plain language and get the step from the manual, safety bulletin or procedure that applies, with the source attached.

  • 02

    Control-room runbooks

    Operators ask about switching procedures and outage runbooks and get the passage that answers them, with a confidence score.

    Answer customers on your site or app from the same approved content your call center uses. Low-confidence answers go to a fallback you set.

Governance

Governed, out of the box

By policies you control, in every deployment.

  • Per-tenant isolation

    Separate keys per tenant. Your prompts, documents and outputs stay in your tenant and are never used to train models.

  • Encrypted in transit and at rest

    TLS 1.2+ on all network traffic. Data at rest under AES-256. NeuralSeek integrates with your own key management service.

  • No training on your data

    Your data stays yours. Neither NeuralSeek nor its subprocessors use your prompts, documents or logs to train models.
