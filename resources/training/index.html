Official certification

Certification tracks for your team

Built and delivered with a specialist training partner. Each track builds on the one before, from a shared vocabulary to agents in production.

  • Orientation and overview

    Available now

    Everyone involved in scoping an agent project, from business owners to engineers.

    The foundational track, which gets a whole department using the same words. It covers the problems modern AI systems have, hallucination first among them, and how grounding answers in a knowledge base of approved material solves them. A second course covers explainability, bias, privacy, regulatory exposure and intellectual property.

    You will be able to

    • Understand how agentic AI changes day-to-day work across functions
    • Share a vocabulary for scoping and governing an agent project
    • Apply platform best practice when planning an implementation
    • Recognize where an agent adds value, and where a guardrail is required first

  • Foundation and core workflows

    Coming soon

    Practitioners who will build and configure on the platform.

    The core technical track: building, configuring and shipping. It starts with the knowledge base, where accuracy is decided: the retrieval techniques available and how to design the ingestion pipeline that fills it. It then moves to agents and mAIstro workflows, with learners building agents of their own.

    You will be able to

    • Build and configure your first agent end to end
    • Connect a knowledge base and check retrieval quality
    • Use semantic scoring and grounding to improve answers
    • Apply baseline guardrails and configuration controls

  • Hands-on labs

    Coming soon

    Teams deploying agents into a regulated domain.

    Learners build their own solutions: configuring a knowledge base, setting up a retrieval flow and creating a workflow in mAIstro. Domain-specific paths let a banking team and a clinical team work through the controls and patterns their own reviewers will ask about.

    You will be able to

    • Implement agentic patterns for a specific industry
    • Apply financial-services controls and workflows
    • Build deployments that mask protected health information before the model call
    • Map a domain requirement to a platform configuration

Certificates of completion

Certificates earned by building

Separate from the official tracks. Each certificate is awarded for practical work you have built and shipped.

These are certificates of completion for NeuralSeek training. For NeuralSeek's own SOC 1, SOC 2 Type II and HIPAA compliance, see our certifications page.

  • NeuralSeek Challenges

    Awarded for completing the Challenges program: feature-based exercises that teach the principles of agentic AI by having you implement them. Recipients have built working solutions and hold the foundations for more advanced work.

    Start at the learning portal

  • mAIstro Challenge

    The advanced certificate. Awarded for independently designing, developing and deploying agentic solutions on NeuralSeek, assessed through practical exercises and a full practicum. It shows you can turn a complex requirement into a working system.

    Join the community

  • Business and strategic AI implementation

    Earned through the internship program. It recognizes the ability to evaluate a real business opportunity for AI, through product research, competitive analysis, a data assessment and a plan, and then deliver an agentic solution as part of a team.

    Ask about the internship

Internship

A remote internship for students

A part-time program for undergraduate and graduate students worldwide. Interns design and ship an agentic solution on mAIstro.

The program and how to apply

  • How it runs

    A weekly live cohort call, an individual build track and a small intern team working on a solution together, five to ten hours a week.

  • What you leave with

    NeuralSeek Certificates 1, 2 and 3, and a solution you built with a team and can demonstrate, from business problem to working system.

Questions about training

Cost, enrollment and the internship.

  • 01

    Is this about NeuralSeek's own compliance?

    Training covers courses, certificates and the internship. NeuralSeek's own SOC 1, SOC 2 Type II and HIPAA compliance is on our certifications page.

  • 02

    What does the training cost?

    The certification tracks are delivered with a training partner, and pricing depends on how you buy NeuralSeek. Ask your account contact, or contact us and we will scope it with everything else. Certificates of completion cost nothing but the work.

  • 03

    How do I apply for the internship?

    On the internship page, which covers the program, the open cohorts and work-authorization requirements. The application form is on that page, and you will need your résumé as a PDF or Word file.
