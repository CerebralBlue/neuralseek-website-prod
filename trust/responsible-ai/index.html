Questions risk teams ask

Short answers on accuracy, agents and your data.

  1. 01

    How do we know an answer is accurate?

    +

    Each answer carries confidence scores and shows the source passages it came from. Governance flags hallucinated terms so your team can review them.

  2. 02

    Do agents get the same controls?

    +

    Yes. Agents built in mAIstro include prompt-injection protection and profanity guardrails, and their runs are logged for review.

  3. 03

    Is our data used to train models?

    +

    No. Neither NeuralSeek nor its sub-processors use customer data to train models.
